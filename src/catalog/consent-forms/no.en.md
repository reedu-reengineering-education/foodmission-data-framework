# Do you want to participate in the FOODMISSION Pilot Phase of FOODMISSION?

**Purpose of the project**

You are invited to participate in a research project where the main purpose is to engage citizens as agents of change for sustainable food system transition. We aim to do this with a novel gamified virtual platform which will both collect data and educate and motivate citizens to take more sustainable food choices. In order to develop and improve the platform we invite citizens to participate in the pilot phase and use the platform for six months if possible on a daily to weekly basis.

This project is funded via the EUs HORIZON-CL6-2024-FARM2FORK-01-6, grant number 101181774.

The ethical approval was submitted at ESHCC (Erasmus School of History, Culture and Communication), Erasmus University Rotterdam, application number Ethics ETH2526-0529.

**Why are you being asked to participate?**

We are conducting a first pilot phase of the interactive platform FOODMISSION with citizens that have interest in sustainable food behaviors. The participants are between 18- and 99-years old and are recruited by the individual project partners in Norway, Italy, Netherlands, Slovenia, Germany and Greece. We aim to have 500 participants in total in the pilot phase.

**Which institution is responsible for the research project?**

Western Norway University of Applied Sciences (HVL) is responsible for the project (data controller). re:edu (Germany), University of Thessaly (Greece), Spherical Pixel (Spain), University of Verona (Italy), Erasmus University Rotterdam (Netherlands), CRS System Solutions (Poland) and CCIS-CAFÉ (Slowenia) are joint data controllers and official partners in the project.

**Participation is voluntary**

Participation in the pilot phase of the project is voluntary. You can stop your participation at any time without giving a reason. There will be no negative consequences for you if you chose not to participate or later decide to continue your participation.

**What does participation involve for you?**

A central part of the project is to develop a platform that helps citizens to make sustainable food choices. The purpose of the pilot phase is to gain insight into the usability of our interactive platform FOODMISSION and the sustainable food behavior choices and changes of citizens that are using FOODMISSION. For that, we will ask you questions about your FOODMISSION usage experience, opinions concerning food behaviors and how you personally come to food choices. Via the platform itself, we will gather information about your food choices, learning progress and reached goals

We will also ask about more general information. This includes your username, email and password. We also ask you to indicate additional information on a voluntarily basis: year of birth, country of residency, region of residency, postal code, gender \[male, female, other, unspecified\], level of activity \[sedentary, light, moderate, active\], annual income \[ below\_150000, from\_1500000\_to\_299999, from\_300000\_to\_499999, from\_500000\_to\_699999, from\_700000\_to\_999999, from\_1000000\_to\_1499999, above\_1500000\], education level \[ no\_formal\_education, primary, secondary, vocational, bachelors, masters, doctorate\], dietary preferences \[vegan, vegetarian, pescatarian, gluten\_free, dairy\_free, nut\_free, halal, kosher \], shopping responsability \[no\_specific\_answer, mostly\_me, shared\_equally, mostly\_someone\_else\].

The pilot phase will take six months in total. Each usage of FOODMISSION is up to you. The FOODMISSION app will remind you once a week in case you did not use it. You can opt-out for each question proposed to you.

In return for your participation, we will give you the opportunity to join an information session (online or live), receive a small FOODMISSION merchandise gift and/or small vouchers from third parties (e.g., restaurants, supermarkets).

**In-depth information about privacy**

**Your personal privacy – how we will store and use your personal data**

*   We will only use your personal data for the purpose(s) specified here and we will process your personal data in accordance with data protection legislation (the GDPR).
*   At Erasmus University, we conduct scientific research. We do this to learn, help people, and contribute to society. Since we are an academic institution conducting scientific research, we process your personal data exclusively for research on the basis of public interest.
*   Your name and contact details will be replaced with a code. The code and contact details are only used to send you the link for the app and for internal reference for your data on the server. All data will be anonymized and aggregated for later use.
*   The data will be collected and stored on the Hetzner Server and anonymized data sets will be transferred to secured OneDrive accounts when needed for scientific publications.
*   No personal data will be publicly available.

**How do we analyse and process your personal data?**

We will process your personal data based on Public interest (General Data Protection Regulation art. 6 nr. 1 letter 2), jf. art. 9 nr. 2 letter j). In case you opt out and stop using the FOODMISSION app, we will only process data that was collected until this point in time.

**What will happen to your personal data at the end of the research project?**

The planned end date of the project is 31.07.2029. All personal data will then be anonymised (deleting or rewriting identifiable data), personally identifiable information will be removed, re-written or categorized.
