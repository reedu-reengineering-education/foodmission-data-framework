# Do you want to participate in the FOODMISSION Pilot Phase of FOODMISSION?

**Purpose of the project**

You are invited to participate in a research project where the main purpose is to engage citizens as agents of change for sustainable food system transition. We aim to do this with a novel gamified virtual platform which will both collect data and educate and motivate citizens to take more sustainable food choices. In order to develop and improve the platform we invite citizens to participate in the pilot phase and use the platform for six months if possible on a daily to weekly basis.

This project is funded via the EUs HORIZON-CL6-2024-FARM2FORK-01-6, grant number 101181774.

**Why are you being asked to participate?**

We are conducting a first pilot phase of the interactive platform FOODMISSION with citizens that have interest in sustainable food behaviors. The participants are between 18- and 99-years old and are recruited by the individual project partners in Norway, Poland, Italy, Netherlands, Slovenia, Germany and Greece. We aim to have 500 participants in total in the pilot phase.

**Which institution is responsible for the research project?**

Western Norway University of Applied Sciences (HVL) is responsible for the project (data controller). re:edu (Germany), University of Thessaly (Greece), Spherical Pixel (Spain), University of Verona (Italy), Erasmus University Rotterdam (Netherlands) CRS System Solutions (Poland) and CCIS-CAFÉ (Slowenia) are joint data controllers and official partners in the project.

**Participation is voluntary**

Participation in the pilot phase of the project is voluntary. You can stop your participation at any time without giving a reason. There will be no negative consequences for you if you chose not to participate or later decide to withdraw.

**What does participation involve for you?**

A central part of the project is to develop a platform that helps citizens to make sustainable food choices. The purpose of the pilot phase is to gain insight into the usability of our interactive platform FOODMISSION and the sustainable food behavior choices and changes of citizens that are using FOODMISSION. For that, we will ask you questions about your FOODMISSION usage experience, opinions concerning food behaviors and how you personally come to food choices. Via the platform itself, we will gather information about your food choices, learning progress and reached goals

We will also ask about more general information such as your age, gender, technical background, occupation etc.

The pilot phase will take six months in total. Each usage of FOODMISSION is up to you. You can opt-out for each question proposed to you.

**Brief information about privacy**

We will only use the information about you for the purposes we have described in this letter. We process personal data confidentially and in accordance with the privacy regulations. You can read more about privacy below.

# In-depth information about privacy

**Your personal privacy – how we will store and use your personal data**

*   We will only use your personal data for the purpose(s) specified here and we will process your personal data in accordance with data protection legislation (the GDPR).
*   The data will only be processed by staff employed by the responsible institutions in this project (see above). This means that identifiable data (recordings, notes and interviews) gathered in the Dutch pilot will only be available to researchers from Erasmus University Rotterdam (EUR)). Data or findings can be shared with the other partners in the project, but this data will be completely anonymised.
*   Your name and contact details will be replaced with a code. The list of names, contact details and respective codes will be stored separately from the rest of the collected data. The code and contact details are only used to send you the link for the app.
*   The data will be collected on via your own device and stored on the EUR-secured OneDrive account.
*   No personal data will be publicly available.

**What gives us the right to process your personal data?**

We will process your personal data based on Public interest (General Data Protection Regulation art. 6 nr. 1 bokstav 2), jf. art. 9 nr. 2 bokstav j).

**What will happen to your personal data at the end of the research project?**

The planned end date of the project is 31.07.2029. All personal data will then be anonymised (deleting or rewriting identifiable data), personally identifiable information will be removed, re-written or categorized.
