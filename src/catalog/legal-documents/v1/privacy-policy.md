# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-20

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the FoodMission Platform processes personal data in connection with the citizen science data framework, educational features, and gamified activities.

This policy is designed around the project context described in the FOODMISSION proposal, including GDPR alignment, FAIR data practices, and data protection by design.

## 3. What personal data we process

Depending on your participation and enabled features, we may process:

### 3.1 Account and profile data
- Name or display name
- Email address
- Account identifiers (including authentication identifiers)
- Country and language preferences
- Optional profile information (for example age range, household context, or sustainability preferences)

### 3.2 Participation and research interaction data
- Survey responses
- Focus group and T-Lab participation records
- Consent records and legal acceptance history
- Activity logs in educational and gamified modules

### 3.3 Food-practice and related behavioral data
- Food purchase and consumption entries
- Food waste-related entries
- Sustainability activity inputs and challenge progress
- Dashboard interaction metrics

### 3.4 Technical and usage data
- Device type and operating system
- IP address and session metadata
- Application events and timestamps
- Basic geolocation context where enabled or required for specific features

The Platform currently uses only strictly necessary technical storage mechanisms (for example session/authentication cookies or equivalent tokens) required for core operation and security. We do not currently use analytics or marketing cookies/tracking technologies.

If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and implement any required consent mechanism before enabling non-essential tracking.

### 3.5 Audio and text materials in specific research activities
- Audio recordings and transcripts from interviews, focus groups, or co-creation sessions, where applicable and consented

We do not intentionally request or process special categories of personal data (Article 9 GDPR) as part of standard Platform use.

If a specific research activity could involve special-category data, we will only process it where a valid Article 9 condition applies, with additional safeguards (for example data minimization, restricted access, and enhanced confidentiality controls), and with clear participant information provided in advance.

## 4. Why we process data (purposes)

We process personal data to:

- Provide and secure user access to the Platform.
- Operate citizen science features and data contribution workflows.
- Analyze food-practice data for project research and impact evaluation.
- Generate individual and collective dashboards and reports.
- Improve educational and gamified activities.
- Ensure data integrity, prevent misuse, and maintain system security.
- Meet legal, regulatory, and audit obligations.

## 5. Legal bases under GDPR

For this publicly funded EU research project, our primary legal basis is performance of a task carried out in the public interest (Article 6(1)(e) GDPR), as defined in project governance and applicable law.

Depending on the activity, we use the following legal bases:

- Platform account access, authentication, and service operation: Article 6(1)(e) (public interest task).
- Citizen science participation workflows and research analytics: Article 6(1)(e) (public interest task).
- Optional research activities that require separate opt-in (for example specific interviews or additional optional modules): Article 6(1)(a) (consent).
- Security monitoring, abuse prevention, and incident response: Article 6(1)(e), and where required by law also Article 6(1)(c) (legal obligation).
- Legal compliance, audits, and mandatory disclosures: Article 6(1)(c) (legal obligation).

Where consent is used, you can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

If we rely on a different legal basis for a new purpose, we will update this Privacy Policy before that processing starts.

## 6. Data sharing and recipients

Personal data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis and under appropriate safeguards with:

- Authorized project partners acting as controllers, joint controllers, or processors depending on activity.
- Technical service providers hosting or operating Platform infrastructure.
- Research or oversight entities where required by law or funding obligations.

Data sharing between partners is governed by data sharing agreements and applicable contractual safeguards.

## 7. International data transfers

The project context includes cooperation between EU and EEA participants, including Norway.

At present, personal data processing for the Platform is intended to remain within the EEA.

If a future processing activity requires a transfer to a country outside the EEA, we will only transfer data where a valid GDPR transfer mechanism applies, such as:

- an adequacy decision under Article 45 GDPR, or
- the European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) under Article 46 GDPR, together with supplementary measures where required.

If such transfers occur, this Privacy Policy will be updated and you may request information about the applicable safeguards.

## 8. Data minimization, security, and confidentiality

We apply technical and organizational measures including:

- Access controls and role-based permissions.
- Encryption in transit and at rest where appropriate.
- Pseudonymization where possible.
- Logging and monitoring for security and integrity.
- Controlled environments for research storage and processing.

Only authorized personnel can access personal data for approved purposes.

## 9. Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, legal obligations, and project requirements.

Retention periods by data category:

- Account and operational personal data: deleted when your account is deleted.
- Security and access logs: retained for up to 90 days, unless a longer period is required to investigate a specific security incident.
- Identifiable research raw data: retained only until the end of the project (30.06.2028), unless applicable law requires longer retention.
- Legal consent records: retained for the duration of the project (until 30.06.2028), unless applicable law requires longer retention.

Where analysis can be performed without direct identifiers, we apply pseudonymization and/or anonymization at the earliest practical stage.

After the retention period ends, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

## 10. Anonymized and aggregated outputs

The Platform may produce aggregated and anonymized datasets, reports, and scientific outputs.

These outputs are used for research, policy, and educational purposes. Personal data is not intentionally published in open datasets.

## 11. Your rights

Under GDPR and applicable law, you may have rights to:

- Access your personal data.
- Rectify inaccurate data.
- Erase data in certain cases.
- Restrict processing.
- Object to certain processing.
- Data portability (where applicable).
- Withdraw consent at any time where consent is the legal basis.
- Lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

To exercise rights, contact us using the details in Section 2.

## 12. Automated decision-making

The Platform may use automated scoring or progression logic for gamified activities and dashboards. It is not intended to produce legally significant or similarly significant decisions about users.

## 13. Children and vulnerable participants

The project design does not target vulnerable groups by default. If minors or other protected groups are included in a specific activity, additional consent and safeguards are required.

## 14. Policy updates and versioning

We may update this policy over time. Material changes and new versions may require renewed legal acceptance in the Platform.
